2. Signals, plans and your balance

  • Opening a setup uses 1 signal from your balance.
  • New members receive a one-time welcome allocation of free signals. It is not renewed.
  • Signals you buy in a pack do not expire. They have no cash value, cannot be transferred or sold, and cannot be converted back into money.
  • A 30-day plan (Signals Plus or Signals Pro) adds its signals when your payment is confirmed and switches on its notifications, and for Pro the MT5 connection, for 30 days. When the plan ends, those features stop and the signals you already received stay in your balance.
  • MT5 connectivity does not by itself guarantee that any trade is placed or closed.
  • We may change features, prices and daily limits over time. A change does not take away signals you have already been credited.

3. How payment works

  • You choose a pack or plan and pay by automatic USDT (TRC20 or BEP20), by Naira bank transfer, or, where offered, by manual USDT. Automatic USDT payments are confirmed on the network and your signals are added on their own. For a Naira transfer, our team checks the credit in our bank account and adds your signals once it is confirmed. You do not need to send a screenshot, receipt or transaction ID for a normal payment.
  • If we cannot match a payment to your order, or something does not add up, we may ask you for the transfer reference, a receipt or other proof. Quote your order reference when you contact us. For manual USDT, send us the transaction ID as the order page asks. We cannot promise how long a check takes.
  • Pay the exact amount, to the exact address or account shown for your order, on the exact network shown, and for a Naira transfer type the narration shown for your order exactly as given. A payment of a different amount is not added automatically; it is checked by hand and may be delayed. Funds sent on the wrong network, to the wrong address, or for a different amount may be lost or delayed, and we are not responsible for that.
  • Any network, exchange or bank fees are yours. If a fee leaves us with less than the price, we may ask for the difference before adding your signals.
  • Naira prices follow the exchange rate shown when you start your order.

All payments for signals, packs and plans are non-refundable once paid. This includes unused signals, plans you stop using or that run out, and results that did not meet your expectations, as well as a change of mind.

We will still put right a real mistake on our side:
