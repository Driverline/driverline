3A. Copy trading and the MT5 connection

  • Copy trading copies the trades our team chooses to flag onto your MT5 account, sized by your own settings and refused when they would risk more than the limit you set. It is included in Signals Pro, Signal Vault, Signal Vault Lite and the Copy Trading plan, and in Signals Plus only for accounts linked to our partner programme, where it replaces signals while it is switched on.
  • Unlike signals, Copy trading expires. The MT5 connection that Copy trading needs runs on a server (VPS) that is rented for a fixed period and must be renewed when that period ends, whether or not it was used. So the days of Copy trading, and of the MT5 connection, in a purchase or plan are valid only for that period. If you do not use them, they expire when the period ends and are not rolled over, extended or refunded. Renewing adds a new period.
  • Signals are different: they stay in your balance until you use them. On Signals Plus with a linked account, signals are set aside pro rata only for the days you actually use Copy trading (about 550 signals divided by 30 days for each day); on the days you do not use Copy trading your remaining signals stay valid until used.
  • Copied trades are placed automatically on your account while Copy trading is on. Fills, prices and results will differ from ours, orders can be refused or delayed, and you can lose money, including more than you expect. Nothing here is a promise of profit.
  • If you ask us to connect your MT5 account, you send us your login details and give us permission to use them to set up your connection. The password is stored encrypted, only our administrators can see it, and it is deleted when the connection is set up. A trading password gives full control of the account, so share it only if you accept that, and consider changing it afterwards. You can ask us to disconnect your account at any time.
