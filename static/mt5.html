Connect your MT5

1. Add an account here (for example Demo, Live or Swap Free) to get its key. 2. Put the TradeLens EA on a chart in that MT5 terminal on a PC or VPS. 3. Paste your TradeLens address and that account's key into the EA inputs. Each MT5 account needs its own key, so a Demo and a Live account are two accounts with two keys. 4. In MT5: Tools, Options, Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest and add your TradeLens address. The EA never opens a trade on its own. It opens, closes or adjusts a trade, or places a pending order, only when you click a button in the app and have allowed that in its settings (AllowExecute, AllowRemoteClose, AllowManage, AllowPending).
