Privacy Notice

TradeLens, operated by Driverline. Effective 7 November 2026. Updated 8 November 2026 to describe the Deriv client ID.

1. Who we are

TradeLens is an independent trading-analysis service operated by Driverline. It is not owned, operated or endorsed by Deriv. This notice explains what personal data we collect, why, who receives it and what choices you have. Contact us through the support details on the Billing page or in the Terms.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your e-mail address, a hashed password (we never store your password in readable form), your plan, and the dates you joined and signed in.
  • Use of the service: how many analyses and questions you use, signals and notifications you receive or open, your preferences, messages in your inbox, and the questions you type into the assistant.
  • Trading data you choose to connect: if you connect an MT5 account or the trade journal, the account number, server, balance, positions and trade history reported to us. If you sign in with Deriv, your Deriv account ID and your Deriv client ID, which we read from Deriv using the sign-in you just authorised and use only to match our own records to Deriv Partners Hub. We do not keep your Deriv access token and we do not place trades through that sign-in.
  • Payments: orders, amounts, transaction references and payment status. We do not receive your card or wallet secrets.
  • Referrals and rewards: your referral code, who invited you, your points and their history.
  • Partner programme (when it is open): your partner application and status, the Deriv partner ID you give us, your Deriv referral link, the records of members introduced by you, the date and version of each notice you consented to, hand-off events (the moment you chose to continue to Deriv), and an administrator's audit notes. A TradeLens record is never proof of Deriv attribution, and Deriv alone decides partner approval, client tagging and commissions.
  • Where you came from: the campaign or referral link you arrived through, so we can measure sign-ups.

3. Why we use it, and our basis

  • To provide the service you asked for, take payment and give support (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and keep accurate referral and payment records (our legitimate interests, and legal obligations where they apply).
  • To record your consent before we send you to Deriv, or before you apply as a Deriv partner, and to keep proof that you gave it (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time for future use).
  • We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

4. Who receives it

  • Hosting and database: Render and Supabase host the application and its database in Europe.
  • AI provider: Anthropic receives the market context and the questions you type into the assistant, to produce answers and Trade Cards. Please do not type personal or confidential details into it.
  • Payments: NOWPayments handles cryptocurrency payments. Bank transfers are checked by us from the references you provide.
  • Messaging: Telegram, if you link the Telegram bot, and your browser vendor's push service, if you turn on notifications.
  • Deriv: when you choose to continue to Deriv, Deriv's own website opens and Deriv's privacy policy and terms apply from that point. We send Deriv no personal data from TradeLens, apart from what is in the official referral link itself. If you sign in with Deriv, Deriv confirms your account ID to us, and we ask Deriv's user-information service for your Deriv client ID.
  • We share data with authorities only where the law requires it.

5. Cookies and similar storage

  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in.
  • Short-lived cookies used for Deriv sign-in security.
  • Cookies that remember a referral code or campaign for up to 30 days (first one wins), so a referral is recorded correctly when you sign up. You can delete them in your browser.

We do not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes above. Payment, referral, consent and audit records may be kept longer where we need them for accounting, security or to show what you consented to.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose, and you can withdraw consent. Contact support from your account e-mail. We may keep what we must keep by law or to protect against abuse, and we will tell you what that is. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security and international transfers

Data is stored with access controls, and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure. Some providers listed above may process data outside your country; we choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

9. Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows the latest version. Where a change matters to a consent you gave, we will ask again.

Education and risk analysis only, not financial advice. Trading carries a high risk of loss.
