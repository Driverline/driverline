About this programme

TradeLens is independent of Deriv. It is not owned, operated or endorsed by Deriv. Deriv alone decides partner approval, client tagging and commissions. TradeLens only keeps its own referral records and routes members to official Deriv referral links. A TradeLens referral is not proof of Deriv attribution, and TradeLens does not share any Deriv commission or give points or rewards for Deriv sign-ups, partner recruitment, trading volume or trading results.
