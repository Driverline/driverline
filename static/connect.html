TradeLens Connect (Unofficial)

An Android Telegram app with TradeLens built in. Free to download.

Get the app

Chat on Telegram as usual, and if you are a TradeLens member, open your TradeLens dashboard and signals without leaving the app.

Download the Android app (APK)

Works on Android 10 to 12, on 64-bit phones. A version for Android 13 and newer is being prepared. Each release page also lists the file's SHA-256 checksum so you can check your download.

How to install

  1. Open the download link above on your phone and download the file that ends in .apk.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. If Play Protect shows a warning for an app it has not seen before, choose Install anyway. You can also check the checksum first.
  4. Open TradeLens Connect and sign in with your phone number, exactly as in any Telegram app.
  5. Optional: open the TL Comm tab and tap your TradeLens card to link your TradeLens account.

What you get

  • A full Telegram app: chats, groups, channels, media, calls, and your existing Telegram account.
  • TL Comm: the official TradeLens bot, channels and community group in one tab.
  • TradeLens dashboard (members): your plan, usage and features, read from TradeLens.
  • Signals (members): the latest setups, with the same allowance rules as the website.
  • Signal alerts in Telegram (members who turned alerts on): sent by the TradeLens bot.

Your data

  • You can use the app as a normal Telegram app without a TradeLens account. Linking is optional.
  • When you open TradeLens for the first time, the app asks you first. Telegram then shares your name, username and Telegram ID with TradeLens, as it does when you open any bot's web app.
  • Your Telegram messages, contacts, password and login codes are never sent to TradeLens, and Telegram data is never used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Read the TradeLens Privacy Notice and the Terms.

About Telegram

TradeLens Connect is an unofficial Telegram app. It uses the Telegram API and is built with Telegram's open-source technology (TDLib), based on the open-source Telegram X app. It is not made, owned or endorsed by Telegram, and "Telegram" is a trademark of its owner. TradeLens Connect works with users of the official Telegram apps and other Telegram apps.

Cost

Downloading and using TradeLens Connect is free. Paid TradeLens plans and tokens are sold by TradeLens through the TradeLens website, at the usual prices. The app shows no advertising of its own. Like every Telegram app, it may show Telegram's own sponsored messages in public channels.
